# Strategy one-pager

Copy this, fill it in with the creator, and keep it short enough to read in a minute.

```
CREATOR:       [name]
DATE:          [date]
GOAL:          [audience / leads / authority / income / community, in their words]

THE VIEWER
[One sentence. Situation, what they are trying to do, what is in the way.]

THE PROMISE (under 15 words)
I help [viewer] [result] by [way].

THREE PILLARS
1. [topic]   why it serves the promise: [...]   proof: [...]
2. [topic]   why it serves the promise: [...]   proof: [...]
3. [topic]   why it serves the promise: [...]   proof: [...]

PROOF INVENTORY (things they can show or point to)
- [...]
- [...]

THE ONE CALL TO ACTION
[verb] [thing] at [place]

HUB / SPOKES (from the intake)
Hub: [platform]   Spokes: [platforms]   Hours per week: [n]

STOP LIST (next 90 days)
- [...]

90-DAY TARGET (things the creator controls)
- [posts published]
- [experiments completed]
- [conversations started with the target viewer]

HOW WE WILL KNOW (what we will read, from their own analytics)
- [metric] on [platform], compared with their own median
```
