## LinkedIn

**How people find content here (maintainer summary):** your network widening by engagement; professional intent.

**Maintainer's view (an opinion, not a fact):** Best for B2B leads, hiring and credibility. Text and document carousels do the work.

**Format fit, maintainer priors from 1 to 5 (opinions you can edit in `fit.json`):**

| Format | Fit |
|---|---|
| Short video | 3 |
| Long video | 2 |
| Carousel or document | 5 |
| Text | 5 |
| Live | 2 |

**What to measure:**

- Comments (depth, not count), and how early they arrive
- Impressions per post against your own median
- Profile views and inbound messages
