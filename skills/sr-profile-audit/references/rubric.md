# Profile rubric (100 points)

Score each part, write one line of evidence, add up. Be honest.

## Name and handle (15)
- 5: A stranger can say it and spell it after hearing it once.
- 5: Handle matches or nearly matches the other platforms.
- 5: The name field contains the main search term for what they do (where the platform allows it).

## Promise (25)
- 10: Says who it is for.
- 10: Says what they get, as an outcome, not a job title.
- 5: Under the preview length, so none of it hides behind "more".

## Proof (15)
- 8: One specific number, name, place or result.
- 7: It is true and the creator can show it.

## Link and call to action (15)
- 5: There is exactly one link, and it works.
- 5: The page it opens matches the promise.
- 5: The call to action is one verb and one place ("Get the checklist").

## Visuals (10)
- 5: The avatar is readable at the smallest size it appears.
- 5: The banner, if any, supports the promise and does not repeat the bio.

## Pinned or featured content (15)
- 5: Three pieces are pinned or featured, if the platform allows.
- 5: They show different sides of the promise.
- 5: At least one proves the proof.

## Platform extras (5)
- Highlights, playlists, featured section, about section, contact options, the pieces the platform lets you fill in. One point for each of five that are filled in well.

## Bands
- 85 and over: leave it alone and spend the time making content.
- 70 to 84: tune the weakest part.
- 50 to 69: fix the top three in order.
- Under 50: rewrite the promise first, then everything else.
