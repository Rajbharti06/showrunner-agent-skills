# Hook shapes

_Generated from the Showrunner data files by `scripts/gen_refs.py`. Do not edit by hand. Fix the data and regenerate._

32 structures the scorer recognises. Fill the braces with true details from the creator's own experience. Never invent the number or the story.

| Shape | Template | Good for | Ruined by |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Specific Loss | `I lost {amount or time} because of {small, avoidable thing}.` | Stakes and honesty in one line. The viewer stays to learn the avoidable thing. | A vague loss ('I lost a lot'), or never naming the thing. |
| The Receipt | `In {time} I went from {A} to {B}. One change did it.` | Proof first, method second. Works when the numbers are real and checkable. | Numbers you cannot back up. Hiding the one change until the end. |
| Nobody Tells You | `Nobody tells you {truth about this stage}.` | Insider permission. The viewer feels they are getting what the guides left out. | Using it for something everybody already says. |
| The Contrarian Flip | `{Popular advice} is wrong. Do {opposite} instead.` | Pattern interrupt. Disagreeing with a belief the viewer holds forces a decision to stay. | A flip with no evidence or no better alternative. |
| The Biggest Mistake | `The biggest mistake {audience} make with {topic} is {x}.` | Self-diagnosis. People watch to check whether they are making it. | A mistake nobody actually makes. |
| The Numbered List | `{N} {things} I wish I knew before {milestone}.` | A visible finish line. The viewer knows how long the payoff takes. | Padding to hit the number. A list with no strongest item first. |
| The Statistic | `{X}% of {group} {surprising behavior}.` | A number the viewer wants to be on the right side of. | An invented or unsourced figure. |
| The Compressed Timeline | `How I {result} in {short time} with {constraint}.` | Promises a method inside a constraint. The constraint is what makes it feel possible. | A timeline you did not actually hit. |
| The Warning | `Do not {action} until you know {thing}.` | Loss aversion. A warning is more urgent than a tip. | A warning with nothing at stake. |
| The Hidden Mechanism | `Here is why {phenomenon} really happens.` | Explains something the viewer has seen but could not explain. | An explanation that is just a restatement. |
| Before and After | `Before: {pain}. After: {gain}. The difference was {one change}.` | A clear contrast the viewer can picture. | No believable bridge between the two states. |
| The Callout | `If you are a {specific person} who {specific situation}, watch this.` | Filters in the right viewer and tells everyone else to move on, which raises retention. | A callout so broad it names everyone. |
| The Confession | `I am embarrassed to admit I {did X}.` | Lowers the viewer's guard. Honesty reads as credibility. | A fake flaw that is really a brag. |
| I Tried It So You Do Not Have To | `I tried {thing} for {time} so you do not have to.` | Borrowed experience. The viewer gets the result without the cost. | Reporting a result you did not actually test. |
| Unpopular Opinion | `Unpopular opinion: {claim}.` | Invites agreement or argument, both of which are engagement. | An opinion nobody disagrees with. |
| The Question With Teeth | `Why do {group} always {surprising behavior}?` | A question the viewer cannot answer in their head, so they wait for it. | A yes or no question. A question you answer in the next sentence. |
| What They Will Not Tell You | `What {authority} will not tell you about {topic}.` | Taps distrust of institutions. Strong, but only if the reveal is real. | Teasing a secret that turns out to be common knowledge. |
| The Myth Bust | `You have been told {myth}. Here is what actually happens.` | Replaces a belief with a better one. Strong for educational channels. | Busting a myth nobody believed. |
| The Clock | `You have {time} to {action} before {consequence}.` | A real deadline creates a reason to watch now. | A fake deadline. Viewers learn quickly. |
| The Teardown | `I broke down why {thing} got {number} views.` | Lets the viewer borrow a pattern that already worked. | Praising the thing instead of finding the mechanism. |
| The Shortcut | `The fastest way to {outcome} if you are {constraint}.` | Speaks to impatience and a real limit on time or money. | A shortcut that skips the hard part without saying so. |
| The Cold Open | `{Time and place}. I was {doing something ordinary} when {the unexpected thing}.` | Starts inside a scene. The viewer wants to know what happens. | Scene-setting that takes more than one sentence. |
| Head to Head | `{A} vs {B}: I tested both for {time}.` | Built-in tension. The viewer picks a side before the answer. | A comparison with no clear criteria. |
| The Beginner Trap | `If you are just starting {skill}, avoid {trap}.` | Protects a vulnerable viewer. Trust builds fast. | Warning against something that is not actually a trap. |
| You Do Not Need It | `You do not need {thing people think they need} to {outcome}.` | Removes a barrier. High share rate among beginners. | Telling people they do not need something they really do need. |
| The One Thing | `If I could teach only one thing about {topic}, it would be {x}.` | Forces prioritisation. The viewer gets the 80 percent. | Naming one thing and then listing five. |
| The Exact Process | `This is my exact {process} for {outcome}.` | Specific and replicable, which is rare enough to earn saves. | Calling it exact and then being vague. |
| The Named Rule | `The {N}-{unit} rule for {situation}.` | A memorable handle people can repeat to someone else. | A rule that is really three rules. |
| The Forecast | `By {year}, {prediction}. Here is what to do now.` | Future stakes plus a present action. | A prediction with no action attached. |
| Someone Else's Result | `My {student, client, friend} {result} using {method}.` | Social proof without bragging. Easier to believe than your own win. | Results you cannot name, even anonymously. |
| The Superlative | `The {best or worst} {thing} I have ever {verb}.` | Extreme claims get attention. The story under it earns the retention. | A superlative the story does not support. |
| The Quiet Part | `Nobody says this out loud, but {truth}.` | Permission to hear something uncomfortable. | A truth that is not uncomfortable. |
